about us

Santos Agri —

a Brazilian company based in Luís Eduardo Magalhães, specializing in grain trading and brokerage services. For over 15 years, Santos Agri has connected Brazilian agribusiness with global markets through trust, security, and expertise.

Our roots are in agriculture, and our mission is simple:

Quality, Efficiency, Respect for partners, and Fair Prices

Thanks to a flexible structure and deep knowledge of the local market, we’re able to offer personalized solutions to meet your sourcing needs.

Building trust through
transparency

We believe Brazilian agriculture deserves global recognition — our mission is to be the bridge between the field and the world.

Strong Partnerships,
Lasting Value

We grow long-term partnerships built on transparency, mutual respect, and quality service. By working directly with producers, we ensure full traceability of each batch and maintain competitive prices.

our services

What we do

CULTIVATION

Together with local farmers, we participate in joint cultivation of grains and legumes in Brazil’s rich soils, blending tradition and innovation for global markets.

STORAGE

Best quality crops are delivered to our warehouses for safe storage, further cleaning and tailored to your needs packaging

PROCESSING

Advanced grain processing guarantees purity, consistency, and compliance with international food safety standards.

CARGO DELIVERY

From Brazil to any port worldwide — we provide secure, timely, and fully traceable delivery of your cargo.

    our advantages

    What we are proud of

    Strong Network of Local Farmers

    Access to a large network of local farmers producing specialty crops. We know our suppliers and cherish our partnership

    Fertile Lands of Brazil

    Seeds and grains are grown in Brazil, boasting rich soils and optimal climatic conditions, and this contributes to their exceptional quality

    High Processing & Storage Capacity

    Annual processing and storage capacities – over 3000 metric tons

    Strict Multi-Stage Quality Control

    Multiple grain quality checks - first at the reception stage, and again before each final shipment to customers

    Up to 99.5% Product Purity

    At our facilities, all products are going through multi-level mechanical cleaning, which can guarantee up to 99.5% purity of the produce

    Strategic Location for Global Trade

    Proximity to the river and sea ports, as well as location on the border with the EU, is advantageous in terms of time and logistic costs

    Flexible Packaging & Delivery Options

    We offer flexible packaging (20–1000 kg or bulk), custom branding, mixed shipments, and multiple delivery options to fit your needs and budget.
